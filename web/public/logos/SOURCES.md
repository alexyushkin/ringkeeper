# Logos

Logos are trademarks of their owners, used to identify integrations; no endorsement implied.

Each file here is the owner's or the collection's own, unchanged: it's only scaled on the page, on
a plate that gives it clear space. `-dark` is the version for dark backgrounds. Nothing is fetched
at runtime. A service with no file here shows its initials on its color (`components/catalog.tsx`).
