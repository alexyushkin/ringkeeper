**On a Mac:** the app isn't signed yet, so don't double-click it the first time. In Finder, choose **Go › Applications**, right-click **Ringkeeper**, choose **Open**, then click **Open** again. If there's no second **Open** button (macOS 15 or newer), follow the [install guide](https://github.com/alexyushkin/ringkeeper/blob/main/docs/install-desktop.md#mac-experimental).
