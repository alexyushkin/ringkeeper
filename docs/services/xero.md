# Xero

## How it signs in

Add your Xero app in **Settings › Developer apps**, then sign in from the tile and choose the
organisations. One sign-in can reach several, so a call names one, and a project's link can
be narrowed to one.

## What it can do

- **Read:** profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance, aged receivables and payables, bank
  summary, invoices, bills, contacts, payments, quotes, purchase orders and journals.
- **Change:** contacts, and draft invoices, bills, credit notes, quotes and purchase orders.
- **Money:** approve, void and email invoices, record payments, bank entries, transfers and
  manual journals.

## What's held

- **No write ever runs by itself,** even on a demo company.
- **A new record with no status, or status DRAFT,** waits for your approval. So do contacts.
- **The name typed, in the app:** any other status, approving, editing an invoice, sending,
  voiding, deleting, payments, bank entries, journals, and a contact's bank details.

An edit needs the name because the call can't show the invoice is still a draft.

## Undo

Nothing here can be undone by Ringkeeper. Void or delete the record in Xero instead.
