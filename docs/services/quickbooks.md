# QuickBooks Online

## How it signs in

Add your Intuit app in **Settings › Developer apps**, then sign in from the tile and choose
the company. Each company is its own connection. A sandbox company counts as test data.

## What it can do

- **Read:** profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, trial balance, aged receivables and
  payables, and any record through a search.
- **Change:** customers, vendors, estimates and purchase orders.
- **Money:** invoices, bills, payments, bill payments, expenses and journal entries.

## What's held

- **No write ever runs by itself,** even on a sandbox.
- **Estimates, purchase orders, customers and vendors** wait for your approval. They don't
  touch the books.
- **The name typed, in the app:** invoices and bills, because QuickBooks has no drafts and
  creating one posts it. So do payments, expenses, journal entries, sending, voiding and
  deleting.

## Undo

Nothing here can be undone by Ringkeeper. Void or delete the record in QuickBooks instead.
