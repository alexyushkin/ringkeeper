# Bluesky

## Where to get the key

In Bluesky, open **Settings**, then **Privacy and security**, then
[App passwords](https://bsky.app/settings/app-passwords), and add one. Paste it here with your
handle, like `yourname.bsky.social`. Don't use your account password: Ringkeeper refuses
anything that doesn't look like an app password.

Add one connection per account. It's free, and there's no review.

## What it can do

- **Read:** profiles, an account's posts with their likes, reposts and replies, a post's
  thread, notifications, and search.
- **Post:** a post or a reply, with a link card if you want one. Links in the text become real
  links by themselves.
- **Delete:** a post.

Pictures and video aren't available yet, and neither are direct messages.

## What's held

- **Reading runs by itself.**
- **Posting, replying and deleting need the connection's name typed,** in the app itself. They
  can't be approved from a chat app, and nothing here can be trusted for an hour.
- **Bluesky has no drafts.** A post waits on its approval card until you answer it.

## Undo

A post can't be undone here. Delete it instead.
