# Resend

## Where to get the key

In [Resend](https://resend.com/api-keys), create an API key. It starts with `re_`.
Sending access is enough to send; full access also lets the AI read domains and whether
an email was delivered. The From address must be on a domain you verified in Resend.

## What it can do

- **Read:** sending domains, and one sent email with its delivery status.
- **Change:** send an email, or a test to your own address.

## What's held

- **Every email waits for your approval,** even on test data.
- **More than 10 people at once** needs the connection's name typed, in the app.
- **A test to your own address runs by itself,** if you set one.

## Undo

A sent email can't be unsent.
