# Pipedrive

## Where to get the key

In [Pipedrive](https://app.pipedrive.com/settings/api), click your account name, then Company
settings, Personal preferences and API, and copy your token. It acts as you, with your
permissions.

## What it can do

- **Read:** people, organizations, deals, activities, notes, pipelines and stages.
- **Change:** add or edit people, organizations, deals and activities, and add notes.

An edit can't delete a record, change who can see it, or change someone's marketing status.

## What's held

- **Adding and editing wait for your approval,** even on test data.
- **Deletes and merges** need the connection's name typed, in the app.

## Undo

An edit to a person, organization, deal or activity can be undone. It puts back the main
fields, and a field that was empty before stays as the edit left it. Custom fields aren't put
back.
