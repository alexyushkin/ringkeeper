# HubSpot

## Where to get the key

HubSpot has no pasted key here. Add your own HubSpot app in Developer apps, then sign in from
the tile. Your app's scopes decide what can be reached.

## What it can do

- **Read:** the account, contacts, companies, deals, tasks, owners, pipelines, properties and
  sequences.
- **Change:** add or edit contacts, companies, deals and tasks, and add notes.
- **Reach people:** send a marketing email, or enroll a contact in a sequence.

## What's held

- **Adding and editing wait for your approval,** even on test data.
- **Deletes, merges and bulk edits** need the connection's name typed, in the app.
- **Sending email and sequence enrollment** need the name typed.

## Undo

HubSpot edits can't be undone here.
