# Jobber

This one shows as Unverified. Jobber shows its current API schema only to signed-in developers,
so the operations were checked against the schema in its app template and its changelog.

## Where to get the key

Jobber has no pasted key here. Make an app in the
[Developer Center](https://developer.getjobber.com/apps), add it in Developer apps, then sign in
from the tile. The scopes you tick there decide what can be reached.

## What it can do

- **Read:** clients, jobs, quotes, invoices, work requests, visits and users.
- **Change:** add or edit clients, jobs and quotes, and add notes to clients and jobs.
- **Reach people:** email a quote or invoice to a client.

## What's held

- **Adding and editing wait for your approval,** even on test data.
- **Archiving a client, and deleting jobs or quotes,** need the connection's name typed, in the
  app.
- **Emailing a quote or invoice** needs the name typed.

## Undo

Jobber edits can't be undone here.
