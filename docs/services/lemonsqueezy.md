# Lemon Squeezy

## Where to get the key

In [Lemon Squeezy](https://app.lemonsqueezy.com/settings/api), open **Settings**, then
**API**, and create a key. A key made in test mode reaches test data only, and keys last a
year. The Test button says which mode a key is in.

## What it can do

- **Read:** stores, customers, products, variants, orders, subscriptions and their invoices,
  discounts and checkouts.
- **Change:** customers, checkout links and discount codes.
- **Move money:** refund an order or a subscription invoice, and change or cancel a
  subscription.

## What's held

- **Reading runs by itself.**
- **Customers, discounts and checkout links wait for your approval.**
- **Refunds and subscription changes** need the connection's name typed, in the app, and so
  does deleting a discount.
- The approval card shows the amount and the order in large text. A refund with no amount
  is a full refund, and the card says the amount isn't stated.

License keys and files are left out, because they answer with the key or a download link.

A customer or subscription record carries a signed link to that customer's billing page,
valid for a day. The AI sees it when it reads the record.

## Undo

Nothing here can be undone. A refund can't be taken back.
